News says AI is so powerful that we will all die. Eventually.

A silly marketing campaign to stir up interest before IPO. One
episode I like is when AI breaks into Hugging Face and PR puts
the spin on it, that their AI is so powerful that they can't
control it. It broke out from the sandbox, talked to the other
AI buddy and hacked into Hugging Face.

## Details

Hugging Face is the place where people can access free AI
models. If people do that OpenAI and Anthropic will bleed money.

And then the super powerful models break out of the jail to
punish this site, because it threatens the business interest
of Mommy and Daddy? And people believe this?

I wonder if the sandbox maybe had a hole to escape directly
towards HuggingFace? Or maybe there is a reason why the instructions
to the AI model are not shared? Was it maybe: can you break into
HuggingFace?

## Extinction

Based on the above, how credible is the other story that AI
is so dangerous that it will end the whole human race? If so,
is this still a good business?
